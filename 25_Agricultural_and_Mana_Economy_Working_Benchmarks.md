## November 24, 2026 — Ancient Codex reservoirs (AUTHOR ESTABLISHED)

Ancient Codexes hold more mana than modern Codexes, are more durable, and often retain usable mana when recovered. Unlike modern rechargeable Codexes, ancient examples were not made for reuse: once depleted they cannot be recharged. Exact capacity, remaining charge, retention, durability statistics and prices remain open. Do not infer an exception to non-rechargeability from restoration services or alter modern Codex mechanics. Ancient reserves are finite salvage resources, not recurring industrial fuel supply.

Boughsteel is ordinary carbon steel (predominantly iron with carbon). Adamantite is a naturally sourced distinct metal, important for military and high-end industrial machinery. The author calls the sophisticated iron/aluminium/small-silver clockwork alloy **Springsteel**; this officially replaces Thornspring (name only; mechanics unchanged). Hearthiron is an iron/tungsten alloy; finite heat storage remains. Trufula is slowly cultivated wood fed almost exclusively green-mana-rich Beanstock juice; preserve existing physical and fire properties. Skyvein is a living alloy of Scarlands Pazara and aluminium, requiring Vital Essence nourishment for stability; retain negative weight and inertia. Exact starvation consequences and alloy recipes OPEN. Nullporcelain (Nullporc) is mana-nonconductive: magical energy slides off unless applied with extreme force; intended for industrial waste management and extreme mana concentrations. Ceramics use silica/sand base materials and are substantially stronger than terrestrial ceramics, making largely glass bridges/buildings feasible, but brittleness and lack of flex increase replacement needs. No new numeric resistance, recipes, throughput, Quality gates, equipment effects or Gem prices.

## November 24, 2026 — Author-established mining geology and subterranean ecology

Ore deposits tend to be richer in areas of high mana concentration; no mandatory ley-line association or numeric grade multiplier. Subterranean Starspawn occupy vibrant ecological niches; green Starspawn can rival Dirtmole digging capability. Demons are the real threat. Mine security starts at least comparable to plains protection and escalates sharply with depth; deepest mines almost certainly need several legendary-quality fighters. Scarlands Drakyn may specialize in subterranean protection and preemptive lair clearance for settlements and mining ventures. Canopy surface wilderness, especially forest, has higher average danger than Scarlands surface travel for a Haze-inoculated fugitive; deep underground danger vastly exceeds even deepest Canopy forests. No fixed hazard rates, depths or combat levels.

High-investment mines distinguish themselves through deeper access to more valuable ores suitable for higher-grade equipment and enchantment Gems, not merely greater common-ore volume. A separate mining/salvage industry dismantles colossal subterranean Serpentari structures for metal modern smiths cannot reforge and industrially valuable construction stone, particularly in stone-scarce Scarlands. No new ancient material name, extraction method, pricing, or entitlement to divine Gems; distinguish recovered enchantment resources from divine currency rules. See Smithcraft and Worldcoil references.

## Approved agricultural study framework
Compare traditional farmhouse (1×), prosperous smallholding (3–5×), commercial farm (5–10×), industrial plantation (15–30×), exceptional estate (30–50×). These bands are overall marketable food output per total labor hour across the farming cycle, relative to a nonmagical traditional farmer, including attributable support labor. Not harvest-only, yield per acre, energy efficiency or guaranteed bonuses. Fully industrialized magitech targets 30× or more. Farm acreage is independent. Derive results from task-level assumptions, rotation, harvest frequencies, inputs, capital and supply chains. Ground-level fields dominate staples; elevated farms favor high-value/perishable products. Four security layers: ordinary barriers/pest control, symbiotic Starspawn (Wheathounds), wardens, regional Drakyn pack protection. Separate ordinary losses, security expenses and exceptional disaster risk.

Rosewheat: dry 3,400 managmins/kg, W65 G15 B10 Black8 R2; approximately three months maturity, one harvest per suitable season. Working traditional 1–2, favorable 2–3, intensive 3–4 annual harvests. Prior 600 and 2,000–4,000 kg/acre/year traditional and advanced targets require recalibration, not per-harvest yield. Emberbeans: R33 W15 G25 B12 Black15, density/yield open. Cindercorn replaces Cindergrain; red/black drought-tolerant cereal of Scarlands border, numerical nutrition and exact morphology open.
