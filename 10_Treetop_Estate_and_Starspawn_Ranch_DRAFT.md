The table covers **300 ha equivalent growing/utility area, not 300 ha of canopy shadow**. Earlier [09] proposed unverified effective-to-ground projection ratio 1.0–2.0; realistic actual projected area depends on sun exposure, host tree architecture and crop-specific vertical light competition. Nor is the estate a standard 300-hectare single tract: living-branch beds may be discontinuous, linked by roads, cables, elevators and bridges.

Leshy cultivation uses living trees as medium for eligible crops, **with direct watering**. Tree nutrition and structural impacts remain to be modeled; irrigation water is not automatically supplied by host tree. Leshy proposed planting/preparation ~5 conventional worker equivalents but harvest remains a weakness; specific Craft scale/MP not approved. Equatorial year-round growth does not mean instant maturation or unlimited direct sun.

## 2. Throughput illustrative checks
Assume grain yield **4 t dry grain/ha gross annually**, 3.5 million kcal/t, and **80% delivered-food fraction** accounting for seed/processing/storage (consumer 30% household allowance is a separate demand-side factor).
- 120 ha grains -> **480 tonnes raw grain/year**, **384 tonnes purchased/delivered equivalent**, **1.344bn purchased-food kcal/year**.
- 60 ha roots + 45 ha mixed vegetables/legumes + 30 ha orchard = **135 ha**. Assume **8 million final-delivered kcal/ha/year as very rough blended hypothetical** -> **1.080bn kcal/year**. This rate is especially uncertain for orchards and watery vegetables.
- 30 ha feed x 4 t/ha x 3.5m kcal/t x 0.8 = **336m kcal/year** delivered feed-crop energy in this simplified comparison, **not direct human food**. If a merely illustrative **1.5 kcal cultivated feed per 1 kcal of animal products** (ignoring pasture and other feed) were adequate, this would correspond to at most **224m kcal human food**. **DO NOT treat this as complete or verified animal-feed conversion efficiency.** Physical energy balances require a proper feed, grass, waste and livestock model.
- Delivered edible equivalent including speculative animal output: **1.344bn + 1.080bn + 0.224bn = 2.648bn kcal/year**. Region [07] trial household provisioning demand **14.03686bn kcal/year** -> this estate would supply about **18.9%** of target region's gross household purchase equivalent **IF** the conversion assumptions and crop yields held. **This is not the measured output of a real farm.**
- Contrast estate animal feed at 30ha against separate fortress ranch demands: the estate's 120 tonnes gross feed crop cannot magically feed an arbitrary number of triceratops-sized animals. Grazing, forage imports and other feed lands must be calculated.

## 3. Water and hosting tree capacity (stress test ONLY)
**Illustrative evapotranspiration-equivalent water demand: 4 mm/day over 300 ha** -> 0.004m * 3,000,000 m² = **12,000m³/day**, equal to 12 million liters/day *before* taking account of captured rainfall, crop type, seasonal drought, irrigated vs infrastructure acreage and storage. This is **not** a claim the estate must pump 12 million liters every day: rainfall and host-tree hydrology offset some needs, canopy crop schedules vary, and real engineering needs a separate water budget. A mere 1mm of additional irrigation over 285 cropped ha is **2,850 m³**; pumping it upward is a major task. Water lift energy and pressure distribution need future work.

Separate 15ha infrastructure/nurseries might have lower crop ET; the 300ha 4mm example deliberately rounds up as an envelope illustration, not a daily exact rate.

## 4. Work stages and proposed worker allocation (DO NOT FINALIZE)
- Tree and branch health, soil/substrate eligibility, plant establishment: **Leshy specialists** with ~5:1 conventional efficiency on PLANTING/PLOWING only (design goal).
- Irrigation/pipe/cistern maintenance, water lift, pumps: **dedicated engineers and workers**; some canals and gravity-fed reservoirs could reduce load.
- Weeding, pruning, pest/blight management: Leshy plus helpers; disease handling still costly and time-consuming.
- Harvest: conventional agricultural crews plus **clockwork harvesting**, ladders/cable rigs/bridges/tree-safe pathways.
- Grain drying, threshing, milling, packing and storage: separate food-processing crew, not free output.
- Tree infrastructure repair and fire response: additional workers, guards, materials and capital.
- Noble estate labor allocation: owners can transfer enslaved crews but labor isn't fungible. Training, safety, coercion, relationships, housing and travel costs matter.

**Trial permanent staffing**: 12 Leshy horticultural/planting specialists, 35 general cultivation and irrigation workers, 25 harvest/clockwork specialists, 10 mechanics and water/lift technicians, 12 storage/milling/processing workers, 8 transport/guard/supervision = **102 regular roles**. Includes both free and enslaved workers, with no assumed legal freedom for Leshy. Seasonal harvest peaks may require **additional 30–70 short-term worker assignments** rotated from the region. Roles are illustrative; not a robust labor-days estimate. Note 12 Leshy x 5 planting worker equivalents = 60 on PLANTING tasks, not a universal 60-worker saving or automatic ability to maintain all 300ha.

## 5. Neighboring fortress Starspawn ranch — first ledger, NOT quantified output
Build a separately accounted, defended forest-floor or low-canopy livestock installation. The ranch should not silently be counted within 300ha active crop area. Starspawn are intelligent, remember captivity, and generally fear slaughter. Flesh-producing stock require strong security; some may be triceratops-scale, magical, fire-breathing and organized. Breakouts may create disasters analogous to large predator containment failures.

**Trial ranch cohort for detailed planning:** 20 large meat-oriented Starspawn and 60 smaller or moderate recurring-product producers (milk/eggs/fiber), **not chosen canonical species**. This inventory is illustrative and *does not establish ethical consent or suitable reproduction*.

Ledger headings, currently **unknown** and must be answered before price:
| Cost/output | Required data |
|---|---|
| Feed | kg/day by species, pasture/grazing vs imported energy, 365-day supply and water |
| Security | enclosure strength, magical protection, sentries, fireproofing, emergency repairs |
| Living conditions | shelter, exercise, recovery, medical and population management |
| Recall | ordinary ~24h postmortem body persistence, recall location/unpredictable return; returning stock depleted of fat and muscle |
| Harvest | live transport to town, mortality/processing timing, edible yield per body, unsold flesh vanishes at Recall |
| Nonlethal products | eggs/milk/fibers per year, digestion/magical origin, inventory spoilage |
| Biological inputs | breeding, herd integration, recovery, disease and predators |
| Loss risk | outbreaks, escaped animals, destroyed infrastructure and replacement hunting |

**No numeric meat output** should be calculated yet from imagined triceratops without food-intake, herd reproduction, recoverability and corpse yield data. Likewise avoid presuming all withdrawn farm goods can persist beyond Recall: Starspawn flesh vanishes, assimilated nutrition persists, recurring ingredients may need precise origin rules.
