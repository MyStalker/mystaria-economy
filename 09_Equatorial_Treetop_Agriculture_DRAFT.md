# Equatorial Treetop Agriculture, Leshy Labor, and Vertical Footprint — DRAFT

**Status:** Author-directed landscape and conceptual Leshy capabilities on November 21, 2026. All numerical efficiency, yield, farm area, livestock placement and crop-cycle values are **unapproved scenarios**. See [main-codex Leshen Foundations](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex/blob/main/38_Leshen_Leshy_Foundations.md).

## Approved setting direction and still-provisional Craft benchmarks
- Canopy Kingdoms at **equator**; agricultural potential persists year-round, but daily light cycles, rain seasonality and crop biology still matter.
- **Typical farms are cultivated in giant treetops** in sunlight; forest floor below is **dark and dangerous**.
- Leshy cultivation has woven/fused **wheat, other grains, vegetables, and root crops including potatoes** into living branches, with soil brought up from the floor. Biological mechanism and required substrate mass remain **OPEN**.
- Leshy magic is intended to **replace fertilizer and remedy poor soil**, to permit rare crops under normally bad conditions; powerful Leshy might grow watermelons in desert sand. **This is a design target, not a complete Craft power or exemption from water/light/resource costs.**
- Leshy **planting/plowing efficiency** ballpark at **at least 5 conventional workers per one Leshy**, on those tasks, not overall harvest or whole-farm labor. Leshy can **remove/prevent pests and blight** as a design intention. They are **relatively weak at harvesting**. All magnitudes, time/mana/recovery costs and frequencies need later approval.
- Clockwork harvesters can plausibly help offset harvest bottleneck, but ability to move among branches, platforms, gradients and narrow ways is **not specified**.

## Crucial correction to previous land calculations
[07 Farm Footprint](07_Canopy_Farm_Footprint_DRAFT.md) measured **agricultural productive surface area**, but interpreted 28–40 km² somewhat like conventional flat farmland. In an arboreal economy, distinguish:
1. **Effective sunlit crop area:** aggregate area of actual illuminated plant-growing surfaces, accounting for annual photosynthetic availability.
2. **Ground-projected tree footprint:** horizontal area of canopy that supports these surfaces, which need not match effective cropland.
3. **Total district territory:** settlements, vertical access, guarded underforest approaches, water, forests, pasture/forage and logistical land.
4. **Structural load:** moist soil mass, standing water, roots, grain/fruit mass, residents, carts, machines; tree anchorage and engineered support.
5. **Solar constraint:** vertically stacked growing surfaces cannot all receive unlimited sunlight at the same time. Year-round equatorial growing is not 24-hour daylight and does not itself double output.

Holding older **1,529 ha of active productive cropland** unchanged for an illustration:
| Effective sunlit growing area per ha horizontal canopy projection | Horizontal projection to support 1,529 ha |
|---:|---:|
| 1.0 | 1,529 ha = 15.29 km² |
| 1.3 | ~1,176 ha = 11.76 km² |
| 1.6 | ~956 ha = 9.56 km² |
| 2.0 | ~765 ha = 7.65 km² |

These ratios are pure geometry/productivity scenarios, not a claim that all growing levels get the same sun or water. To earn a ratio >1, account for branch spacing, movable beds, canopy gaps, crop light tolerance and seasonal sun angles, not simply stack soil trays.

**Livestock warning:** Previous [07] assumed **1,000–2,000 ha of pasture/forage meadow**. This needs **fresh investigation**, because a dark/dangerous forest floor and elevated agricultural model may prevent ordinary open-ground pasturing. Alternatives include managed elevated meadows, guarded ground enclaves, imported hay/feed, tethered browsing livestock, and less animal product consumption. None chosen. The old **28–40 km² agricultural footprint is not defensible as total real-world occupied ground area until livestock and structural crop area are reconciled**; retain it only as a flat-land-equivalent *scenario*.

## Labor-efficiency experiment
Earlier [08 Workforce](08_Canopy_Workforce_and_Canopy_Urbanism_DRAFT.md) estimated 420 direct field-crop workers, 380 horticulture workers, 240 animal workers (total 1,040 conventional headcounts) as a deliberately labor-intensive base.

**Illustrative decomposition:** suppose **40% of field labor time** is planting/plowing/preparation across year (this split is invented; real calendar not yet specified). Then:
- Baseline field work = 420 full-year-equivalent worker assignments; planting/preparation 168, other tasks including harvest and field management 252.
- If **one Leshy** covers five planting-equivalents, ~**34 Leshy** cover 168 planting assignments, freeing ~**134 equivalent roles** (168−34) in a full-adoption scenario.
- Total farming workforce under this *narrow change only* = 420−134+380+240 ≈ **906** equivalents, rather than automatically 340 or 208.
- At 50% adoption, ~17 Leshy cover 84 conventional planting equivalents, releasing ~67 equivalents, with field crew ~353 and total direct farm crew ~973.
- This arithmetic uses **worker-time equivalents**, not literal replacement of entire biological populations, and assumes Leshy labor available at adequate times and sufficiently large working areas.
- Pest/blight mitigation and fertilizer substitution may affect **input costs, crop losses, consistency, and productive cultivation on poor soils**, not necessarily headline peak yield. No yield multiplier should be assigned absent Craft rules.
- Year-round planting means **continuous field schedules** and staggered harvest, changing staffing peaks; some crops may allow multiple cycles, but mature tree-crops and grain life cycles must be specified.

**Noble labor relocation:** owners of enslaved labor can move workers among holdings/occupations and seasonally bolster harvest crews; time, distance, rights, equipment, training, food, family disruption, loss of skills and guarding are nonzero constraints.

## Open engineering and economics
- What supports soil mass, growing roots, irrigated beds and loaded clockwork harvesters? Are there viable branch roadways, cable harvesters, or separate machines by crop?
- Soil supply and logistics from hazardous lower forest; how does crop–host tree fusion conserve imported substrate?
- Irrigation: rainfall, interception, storage and pumps; rain shadow from stacked canopy, water-intensive crops and drinking water.
- Darkness and danger on forest floor: are there guarded ranching zones or is animal feed grown in high canopy?
- Does fertilizer-replacement magic work continuously or in treatments? Fertility and crop managmin composition need later mechanics.
- Managmin nutrition remains separate from energy-proxy food accounting.
- Validate river/road supply logistics across vertical urban settlements before approving farm profitability, taxes or currency prices.

## November 21, 2026 — Host-tree substrate and livestock-Starspawn husbandry docket

**AUTHOR APPROVED:** Host trees themselves are the growing medium in most eligible elevated crops; imported soil not universally needed. **Direct irrigation/watering remains necessary** for crops. Host nutrient draw, water distribution, reservoir/pipe/branch transport, drought and infrastructure failure are genuine farm costs and risks. Canon owning document: [38 Leshen Foundations](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex/blob/main/38_Leshen_Leshy_Foundations.md).

**AUTHOR-RAISED QUESTIONS, NOT YET APPROVED AS RULES:** The Canopy's livestock are domesticated Starspawn; dangerous dark forest-floor livestock farms would require defense, but perhaps surprisingly modest defense relative to treetop poultry. Ask whether livestock consent to farming or slaughter, whether lifespan and services are exchanged for protection, whether a domesticated Starspawn returns home after Celestial Recall, whether a single willing cow-like Starspawn can be harvested repeatedly, and how to retain realistic farm threats and economics.

### Canon conflict check (read main-codex 01_World_and_Shimmer.md)
- All Starspawn understand civilized speech and Starsong, most cannot speak mortal tongues.
- Astral Hearts preserve the Cognum, personality and memory through death/Recall. Starspawn can experience pain, fear, confinement and mistreatment.
- After fatal injury, Stillness/Recall may occur; Recall duration **intentionally unspecified**, depending on damage/power/depletion. Neither post-reconstruction return *location* nor voluntary return-to-farm guarantee is established.
- When Recall begins, Astral Heart disassembles remaining original Anima, Spiritus and **Corpus, including physical body**. **It is NOT canon that butchered meat persists after Recall.** Repeated yearly meat harvest therefore cannot be modeled as a free renewable infinite source. What portions are affected after separation/processing/consumption, and how long before Recall, are open questions.
- A Summoner Accord provides access to summoning but is neither universal in livestock nor necessarily voluntary; coercion possible. Do not presume every farmer has an Accord or that Starspawn automatically return home.
- Existing Starspawn ecology already includes grazing and domestication for transport/agriculture.

### Unapproved husbandry design alternatives
A. Conventional captive/breeding meat husbandry, but needs explicit meat-persistence-after-Recall ruling.
B. Long-term voluntary or negotiated husbandry: eggs, milk, fibers, labor, sometimes consent to slaughter, with sentience/Starsong and bargaining central.
C. Renewable annual meat through recurring Recall: must resolve meat persistence, recovery resource/time and body reappearance; risks severe overproduction.
D. Mixed by species and farm: varied eggs/milk/fibers/labor and selective meat under defined exceptions, retain distinct ecological/legal economics.

**Assistant preference for user review only:** D plus elements of B; do not canonicalize animal ethics, sentience thresholds, legal contracts, voluntary slaughter, reincarnation destinations, recovery periods or retained meat without user approval.

**Realistic costs and dangers that should survive any choice:** food/feed crops, pasture or enclosed elevated barns, grazing-guard teams, animal disease, parasites, fertility/reproduction, injuries, birthing, bad weather, manure, crop water, spoilage, medicine, sanitation, building maintenance, transport, predators, Recall disappearance and lost investment, animal memories/relationships, family herd behavior, escape, and legal/political disputes over consent and ownership.

**Agricultural land warning:** Previous 30–40 km² food-production estimate included a speculative 1,000–2,000 ha livestock pasture and insufficiently calibrated feed conversion. Do not declare this footprint stable until canopy vs dangerous ground livestock systems and Starspawn post-Recall meat handling are resolved.
