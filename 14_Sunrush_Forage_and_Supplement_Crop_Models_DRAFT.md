# Sunrush Forage, Leshy Supplements and Mossback Feed-Farm Footprint — DRAFT

**2026-11-21.** User approved the prior Mossback ten-tonne growth table and working numeric feed/throughput model as an economic benchmark. This document tests *new*, unapproved forage yields, nutrition allocations, crop types and acreage; no prices or planting densities are canonical yet.

## Approved baseline inherited from study 13
- Mossback seed ~1.5kg; hatch ~2kg; year 1 350kg; 2 900kg; 5 3t; 8 4.8t; 12 7t; 16 8.8t; 20 10t.
- 12 years to pollination eligibility, 20 years to full size; a maternal Bloom makes the mother shrink and recover on a distinct cycle.
- Modeling feed schedule: age 0–5, 1% live mass/day net dry-equivalent; age 5–12, 0.85%; 12+, 0.75%, **conditional on a working ~50% sunlight-energy offset**. Sunlight's effect on intake, protein, minerals, hydration and micronutrients remains subject to verification.
- Example 24-mother/8-adult support system with 115.2 seeds/year and 75% captive to full size: ~1,933 growing animals across 20 years, ~31,112t/y feed-equivalent consumed, or **37,334 t/y including a 20% operating buffer**. This is a planning benchmark; unverified age-specific losses and 6.25-year cadence affect results.
- Approved Sunrush Grass is vigorous native Canopy bulk forage suppressed by Mossback grazing, but nutrient-dense supplements are needed.

## Research analogues
- Napier/elephant grass agronomic review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10682400/ (mean ~26 t dry matter/ha/year and wide 2–86 t range in the surveyed trials; figures are *total* dry matter before grazing accessibility/field losses and not Sunrush yield).
- Elephant feeding: https://ielc.libguides.com/sdzg/factsheets/asianelephant/diet (elephants consume dry matter ~1.5–2% body mass/day, rather than assuming sunlight offsets nutrition).
- Tropical high-protein forage legume: https://feedipedia.review.fao.org/node/251 (stylo varies widely, often 10–20 t DM/ha/year gross but protein quality and consumable harvest depend on site). Proposed native Mystarian supplement need not mirror exact species.

## PROPOSED feeding recipe, dry-feed equivalent
- **70% Sunrush Grass** (bulk fiber and digestive energy): 26,133.8t/y of buffered demand.
- **25% 'Sunlace Clover' (new, NONCANON species)**: nitrogen-fixing, bright-light tolerant Leshy-managed forage legume; seed/pod-rich harvested green matter used to increase protein and reduce soil fertility decline. 9,333.5t/y buffered demand.
- **5% 'Stonepod Reed' (new, NONCANON species)**: deep-rooted mineral-rich reed grown in moist banks; processed pods/stems and mineral supplements for pregnant/Blooming females, young calves, and salt/mineral balance. 1,866.7t/y buffered demand.
- The 25% and 5% groups can be merged into a single **30% nutrient-dense supplement** for initial land accounting; micronutrient, protein, feed digestibility and managmin adequacy remain unverified.
- Note: 30% supplementation is *not* proof of a precise nutritional formulation. Energy value per dry kg, digestive efficiency, red/green managmin composition, water, seed-stage maternal milk intake and safe feeding of very young Mossbacks require new canon choices.

## Three yield cases, for the proposed 70/30 dry mass mix
'Usable yield' means already accessible and successfully consumed/collected dry-feed-equivalent per hectare-year. Do not double count losses in the 20% operating buffer. Effective illuminated area is not the same thing as forest-floor land footprint, and grass growing on the treetop is not assumed free of sun/water/weight constraints.

| Case | Sunrush usable t/ha/y | Supplements usable t/ha/y | Sunrush ha | Supplement ha | Combined productive ha | km² |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Poor / low-input | 6 | 4 | 4,356 | 2,800 | **7,156** | 71.6 |
| Managed baseline | 12 | 6 | 2,178 | 1,867 | **4,045** | 40.5 |
| Intensive Leshy + irrigation | 20 | 10 | 1,307 | 1,120 | **2,427** | 24.3 |

Compared to the old single-feed 6t/ha case (~6,222ha), a mixed forage crop can appear **less efficient** at some low-input yields due to protein supplementation. That is intended realism, not a contradiction. Improvements require costed inputs, land, water, and labor.

## Water and fertilizer caution
Illustratively, 4mm/day irrigation **equivalent** over the managed baseline 4,045ha is about 161,800,000 liters/day of water delivered from *all* sources before subtracting rainfall. This is crop evapotranspiration equivalent, not a claim that farmers lift/pump the entire amount. Water efficiency, river access, canopy rain, soil retention, and tree roots still matter. Leshy improve cultivation but do not abolish inputs.

## Commercial segmentation
Breeding sanctuary selling age-two young needs ~1,430t buffered feed annually under prior model, but actual species-mix recipe, parent and milk needs, support-herd size, security and veterinary costs require detailed study. The wider 20-year supply chain needs more territory and labor, potentially located across multiple plains growers, Sunrush grazing concessions, and forest finishing sites. Forage management can prevent ecological overgrowth, earning supplemental payments without automatically creating net positive forage profit.

## Explicitly unresolved
Approve/reject new names Sunlace Clover and Stonepod Reed, whether the proposed 70/25/5 diet is nutritionally sensible, the three usable yield cases, managmin feeding, sunlight-energy effect and reconciliation with food dry mass, adult support herd counts, age-specific losses, care labor, water, land rights and price ladder. No universally fixed feed cost or botanical power has been canonized.
