## 2. Food-energy demand (calorie proxy, NOT nutritional canon)

- Small residents: 1,600 kcal/day; Centaurs 18,000; Thornguard 2,800; Leshy zero conventional food; all remain experimental.
- Town intended = 13.12m kcal/day -> gross household-provisioning equivalent 6.84114 billion kcal/year after the existing 30% spoilage/excess allowance.
- Countryside intended = 4,200*1,600 + 300*18,000 + 600*2,800 = **13.8m kcal/day** -> gross household-provisioning equivalent **7.19571 billion kcal/year**.
- Whole district intended = **26.92m kcal/day** -> **14.03686 billion gross household-provisioning kcal equivalents/year**.
- Do not equate this with field-harvest volume: seed, pre-consumer storage/process losses, animal feed conversion, imports/exports and seasonal reserves require a distinct material flow. Financial 10% household food contingency is not a physical yield factor.

## 3. Land use model — illustrative baseline ONLY

Use illustrative retail-side dietary energy split:
- **50% grains and grain-based staples** (7.01843bn kcal). Assume dry grain 3,500 kcal/kg, 4 tonnes gross field output/hectare/year, 80% gross-to-consumer delivery factor -> **~627 ha** direct-grain acreage.
- **30% pulses, roots, vegetables, fruit and other direct plant foods** (4.21106bn kcal). Assume **8 million net purchased kcal/ha/year**, mixed-category scenario only -> **~526 ha** (orchards, vegetables, bean fields; exact diet/yields untested).
- **20% animal-derived food products** (2.80737bn kcal at consumer). Assume **1.5 kcal of cultivated feed crop per 1 kcal animal product** (additional grass, browse, forage support counted separately) and baseline cultivated feed crop yields of 4 t/ha * 3.5m kcal/t * 0.8 net accounting -> **~376 ha** feed crop acreage. This is not a real animal feed-conversion ratio; requires species-specific husbandry and *careful treatment of pasture feed*.
- **Combined active cropland ~1,529 ha = 15.29 km².**
- **Fallow/rotation, soil support, infrastructure and field margins ~230–460 ha**, assumed 15–30% of cropped area, depending on intensity. These are area buffers, not direct energy production.
- **Livestock grazing/forage meadow ~1,000–2,000 ha = 10–20 km²** as rough **additional and unverified** range. Verify pasture output, stocking rates, grass types, imported hay, and land requirements before use. Centaurs do **not** graze pasture like ordinary horses; pasture here is for actual livestock, not Centaur personal feeding.
- **Total agriculturally assigned area ~2,759–3,989 ha = ~27.6–39.9 km²**. Round to **30–40 km²**. It excludes woodland for fuel/timber, substantial roads/settlements, uncultivable slopes, bodies of water, and other ecological land.
- For the **entire mixed landscape**, a working **80–150 km²** settlement and food-production district is plausible as a *design illustration*, NOT a proven ecological carrying capacity. Its implied circle-equivalent radius is about 5.0–6.9 km; practical travel networks and trade catchment can extend much farther than that.

These are model assumptions selected to make a transparent first pass, not calibrated Mystaria magic or a claim about medieval agricultural historical performance. Source context only: [Our World in Data land use](https://ourworldindata.org/land-use), [FAO 2023 cropland per person](https://www.fao.org/statistics/highlights-archive/highlights-detail/land-statistics-2001-2023.-global--regional-and-country-trends/), and [USDA 2024/25 world wheat-yield projection ~3.57 t/ha](https://fas.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/production.pdf). They are not Canopy statistics.

## 4. Productivity scenarios and labor cautions

Realized grain yields must be distinguished from clockwork **labor productivity**. Leshy Craft may improve crop yield, plant survival, water efficiency, number of harvests, or labor, but none has an approved numerical modifier.

Holding the grain side 2,506.6 t gross harvest constant, gross yields and required grain land:
| Gross yield | Area for direct grain |
|---:|---:|
| 2 t/ha | ~1,253 ha |
| 3 t/ha | ~836 ha |
| 4 t/ha | ~627 ha |
| 6 t/ha | ~418 ha |
| 8 t/ha | ~313 ha |

These are not gross land for the entire diet.

Farm counts depend on definition:
- 50-ha *active cropland* unit: 1,529 ha / 50 ≈ **31 cropped-area units**, before pastures/fallow. Not automatically 31 legal estates.
- A noble 300-ha estate can contain cropland, orchards, pasture, villages and managed woodland, and support many workers; its gross area is not the same as actively grain-cropped area.
- Independent smallholder farms might occupy 5–20 ha of mixed land. Specialized Leshy garden operations might be much smaller and more productive per hectare, but exact sizes/productivities are unapproved.
- Number of agricultural workers cannot be obtained solely from total rural population because children, care workers, artisans, unfree/domestic laborers, and trade workers consume time, and clockwork harvesting may sharply reduce labor at certain stages.

## 5. Next empirical/design work

2. Specify overall diet and what percent of total calories are **from food animals** vs directly cropped food; current 20% animal calories is a provisional luxury-diet estimate.
3. Model actual livestock feed requirements from converted crop calories *plus* forage and pasture, and distinguish animal feed losses from household spoilage.
4. Resolve farmland tenure (noble Banner estate; independent freehold; small tenant farms; captured labor, paternalistic Leshy horticulture).
5. Measure gross yield by crops (grains, legumes, roots, orchards) and multiplier from Leshy magic (if approved), mechanized harvesting and actual growing seasons.
6. Measure harvest storage/transport/retailer waste, labor seasonality, local energy/mana inputs, and inventory buffers.
7. Trace harvest to town: household consumption, export surplus, rent/tax in-kind, and scarcity, before predicting prices.
