**As of November 21, 2026.** Author-approved foundations: Canopy freefolk relative prosperity, household A five-person one-earner Thornguard town guard, 10% grocery-cost contingency, high-value Leshy plant work, 100ha Sunrush labor model **as a working benchmark**, worldwide Tidekin enslavement, ordinary Tidekin water moving, specialist industrial groundwater extraction/seawater purification, full ordinary Tidekin irrigation workforce = half equivalent aqueduct in useful delivery. **New silver wage and farm-cost numbers below are deliberately NOT CANON.**

## 1. Existing purchasing power trial from 05 Canopy Household Budgets DRAFT
- Working Thornguard town-guard household A: 240 paid days ×12.5 provisional silver **accounting units/day** =3,000 units/year, ONE household cash income supporting FIVE persons.
- Earlier proposed groceries cost 887.12 silver units/year + author-approved 10% contingency 88.712 => **975.832** /year. **Do not add another contingency**; already included. Separately approved nutrition canon supersedes old household basket's labels "calories/protein"; those quantities may be reinterpreted only as externally calibrated managmin-energy equivalents, not actual in-world calories.
- Other older illustrative annual categories: urban rent450; clothes/household goods225; heat/utilities180; tax240; hobbies300; health/education300 => total **1,695**.
- With food corrected, remaining savings/investments are **3,000−975.832−1,695=329.168 units/year** (10.97% of income), not prior stale 405. This is not a household's mandatory consumption pattern; house purchase within a year is not generally established in urban Canopy, where renting is common.
- No silver coin name/weight, exchange, loan interest or universal wage has been ratified.

## 4. Magnitude of feed bill in this trial
Sunrush previously proposed at 70% of 37,334 tonnes annual planned Mossback-network feed = ~26,134t annually; at ~19.59 silver units per dry tonne *at farm gate*, that would cost ~**512,059 silver accounting units/year** for Sunrush alone, before forage transport, distributor margins and other two fodder crops. This is not the cost of a single Mossback or town; it is the whole twenty-year integrated growing network supporting eventual ~86 adult animals/year. The 70/25/5 proportions were proposed but not individually ratified as mandatory species diets. No currency price officially established.

## 5. Two economically distinct Tidekin skills
1. **Conveyance/distribution**: all Tidekin manipulate extant water subject to their individual Wakecraft rules; a standard 'full workforce' supplies 0.5 full reference aqueduct delivery in approved comparison. Example full aqueduct capacity on test 100ha 4 million liters/day; comparable full Wakecrafter distribution workforce ~2 million L/day, but this *particular* flow figure is solely farm-specific illustration.
2. **Water supply creation from available sources (NOT conjuring)**: specialized Tidekin retrieve large groundwater volumes through appropriate Hydrokinesis and can purify seawater, making formerly unsuitable terrain farmable. Bottlenecks: aquifer recharge, well/source access, seawater delivery, energy/mana/skill limits, storage, brine/salt waste and water ownership. A sourcing specialist is not automatically an extra distribution crew; count the functions separately.
The illustrative 2,100 units/year irrigation account **cannot** be treated as proof that a full source or aqueduct is financed. It is an explicit placeholder requiring a construction/annualized financing study, realistic duty cycle and actual owner/captive arrangements.

## 6. Explicit unresolved decisions
- Is **9 units/day** a reasonable free agricultural worker wage against the earlier guard's **12.5**, or should productivity and living standard imply a different ratio?
- Is 27 units/day sufficient compensation for freely employed Leshy if high-value specialized care is scarce? Existing 5× planting efficiency is approximate task-specific and may be subject to skill.
- Does the illustrative all-in **~16.33 accounting units per tonne dry Sunrush** production cost look right once irrigation and clockwork costs are justified? No named silver coins, weights or established wage rates.
- Would specialized Tidekin sourcing normally serve *multiple* farms as a water utility, a noble aqueduct, or a single large estate? No standard aqueduct cost or industrial gallons/minute assigned.
- Sunlace/Stonepod plant identities, profiles, yields, photosynthetic color fractions and total ration costs pending individual approval.
