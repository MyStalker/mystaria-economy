# Canopy Village Meat Day and Affordable Mossback Steak — DRAFT
Date: November 21, 2026

## Approved directions
- Author: ordinary Canopy folk should have access to **affordable Mossback steak** rather than treat it as a rare noble luxury; explicitly test **community/village meat days** and whether to modestly increase a household food/meat budget. Reference household A is a **five-person, single-earner Thornguard town-guard family**; existing salary 3,000 silver ACCOUNTING units/y (240 days at 12.5), old five-person groceries with approved 10% contingency 975.832 units/y, other old costs total 1,695 units/y, remainder savings 329.168. Silver numbers are a working economic calibration, not minted money.
- Author approved **milk and fur taken from a living Starspawn persist permanently independent of its later Celestial Recall**, including cheese and textile derivatives, unlike dead flesh reclaimed after ~24 hours. Save the moral/cultural symbolism of fur when sourced from speaking animal peoples or living Starspawn; wool can be a more ordinary textile alternative. This does not validate sourcing/consent of any individual coat.
- Milk needed by sprouts is NOT an economic sale; commercial surplus yield, managmin profile and net dairy profit are still hypotheses.

## Model age8: one animal, one town feast
Prior age8 grazing/dairy study22: live mass **4,800kg**, unapproved 40% edible cut yield=1,920kg; hypothetical improved **85% successfully biologically assimilated before Recall** => **1,632kg served+assimilated** from one animal. 400g portion ×**4,000** townspeople=1,600kg, leaving 32kg margin for visitors/etc. At 0.4kg/person, 1,632kg supplies **4,080 portions**.
At mature steady state a 24-mother breeding sanctuary gives expected **98.1504 surviving age8 animals/y**, using still-unapproved intermediary survival. **52 weekly community slaughter days require 52 head/y**, leave about 46 head/y for other demand/markets. The 52-head program yields **84,864kg/year available**; town 4,000×0.4×52=**83,200kg/year actually consumed**, leaving 1,664kg expected slack across the year. A district of 10,000 people at 0.4kg weekly needs about **127.45 such animals/year**, so this same sanctuary cannot supply entire district weekly at that serving size.
This is a cultural & commercial feasibility scenario, **not established every-town weekly calendar**. Meat must actually be digested/assimilated before Recall; not simply frozen/sold. Shared slaughterhouse, butcher halls, communal roast ovens, scheduled household pickup, same-day serving, sufficient daylight and transport can lower losses but 85% utilization not guaranteed. Animal welfare and starspawn personhood persist; a community feast does not imply animal consent.

## Distinguish giant-animal scale economics
Advantages: high per-slaughter output, single slaughter crew mobilization shared across ~1,632kg, high pre-order certainty and town-scale consumption, reduce unsold flesh Recall losses, milk as a separate source of income. Disadvantages: many years of grazing even at age8, expensive breeder/medical care and land/water, danger and handling requirements, one 24-hour Recall deadline, population demand concentrated into narrow distribution window, absent proof of sustainable yield. No cattle-like per-head equivalence or assumption universal farm-gate prices.

## Why simply bumping food budget is not the sole solution
- Give meat-day slaughterhouse **pre-orders**, shared butchery and town-wide scheduled consumers to support a feasible **85%** or better biological assimilation before Recall, subject to 24-hour logistics and adequate food-safety practices. Don't assume 100% utilization, nor prolong Celestial Recall.
- Preserve cheap natural Sunrush pasture as actual feeding channel and income credit from **net surplus** milk; create genuine pasture and rare specialist labor costed ledger, not double-count pasture services.
- Improve/validate sanctuary staffing 44-worker assumption, avoid full market procurement of each nursery specialist, more accurate early milk/managmin biology; cannot achieve 0.70/kg with current approved-scale expenses.
- Target family purchasing power in complete household basket, not cheap price by fiat: existing 3,000 income with 1,695 nonfood costs + 1,260 food leaves tiny savings. Even 1,200 food leaves 105 savings. If author desires e.g. 10–15% savings rate, must reduce other household costs or raise income or restructure ordinary diets.
- Compare actual village demand to population. 4,000 people ×0.4kg/week =83.2t/year; 98 animals/y ×1,632kg =~160.2t capacity. 10,000-person district ×0.4kg/week=208t/y, > network output. (Earlier 200g/week per person for 10k =104t/year; feasible with 98 head at 85%.)
- Investigate **price of cheese and whey** separately, including managmin nutrient ratios, since milk + cheese may displace other purchases and make 'cheesesteak' less expensive or more valued without asserting higher mass conservation.
- A cultural feasting calendar can reduce merchant fragmentation and food waste; not every individual town needs a full-animal slaughter every single week; larger town or multi-hamlet distribution feasible only with credible Recall/digestion timings.
- Ordinary wool may be a more accepted everyday fiber. Source animal options and the broad legal status of fur from sentient races remain future cultural research.
