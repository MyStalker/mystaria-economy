# Mystaria: Currencies and Banking

## Author-established decisions and directions — November 21, 2026

### Divine currency: separate from mundane circulation
- **Gems and Glints** are exclusively usable in divine shops of the gods. A non-Hero who is not a Hero expert generally has no idea what these Gems are. They are not an ordinary retail currency.
- The approved main-codex ratio is **100 Glints = 1 Gem**. Approved patron rewards and Gem Shop item prices remain unchanged.
- Divine Gem prices ideally connect to real-world economic value at some point, but a conversion or valuation mechanism is **not yet decided**.
- A centaur smith can produce a Quality V sword without knowing Gems exist; actual equipment Quality does not depend on using divine shops.

### Three major national currencies
1. **Imperium: Scales.** Paper money reflecting a technologically advanced society. Banking is gold-standard or gold-backed in some form. Exact redemption rules, issuance, reserves and denominations remain OPEN.
2. **Canopy Kingdoms: precious-metal coinage.** Silver is primary, gold secondary. Coin names, weights, minting authority and cross-kingdom interchangeability remain OPEN.
3. **Scarlands: Vitae.** A vital fluid, a highly condensable liquid currency with practical industrial/alchemical utility. It helps power/feed Pazara houses and is foundational to potion craft. It is not gold-standard-based merely because it is currency. People can carry wealth in flasks. Earlier suggestion to name units 'Marks' is **REJECTED/SUPERSEDED**: do not use Marks as the currency name.

### Vitae: color, measurement, source and monetary dynamics
- Vitae is the *essence itself*, denominated/handled by **ounces and colors**, not 'Marks'.
- **Red Vitae** is standard/common.
- **White Vitae** is intermediate.
- **Pure clear Vitae** is the rarest.
- Scarlands alchemists **extract actual Vitae from living people through a torture-like process**. This is productive extraction, not currency counterfeiting.
- Vitae is used/consumed or takes new forms; monetary supply therefore changes with extraction, consumption, conversion and possible loss. Precise chemistry, exchange ratios, yield, extraction constraints, storage, purity testing, or legal oversight are **OPEN**.
- A counterfeiter is an alchemist who creates convincing *non-Vitae substitutes* that pass as genuine Vitae for a lower cost, rather than somebody who extracts genuine Vitae.
- **PROPOSED terminology only:** Express payments as e.g. 'ounces of red Vitae'; precise fractions, acceptable containers and denomination norms have not been approved.

### Two minor currency categories
1. **Ancient Serpentari coinage**, still valuable for constituent precious metal. Composition, rarity, numismatic premium and exchange rates OPEN.
2. **Second minor currency**, identity OPEN. Do not invent it.

## Important unresolved decisions
- Exact silver/gold coin denominations; metal-content standards and redeemability of Scales.
- Economic value of precious metals in different nations.
- Vitae weights/ounces (which local unit standard?), color exchange value, concentration/dilution and verification.
- Relative market values of real Vitae, industrial by-products, labor and ordinary commodities.
- Whether/how divine-shop goods can be sold for mundane money, and institutional response.
- No fixed exchange-rate table or general equivalence between divine Gems and mortal currency has been approved.
