# Mossback Growth Curve and Full Grow-Out Feed Budget — DRAFT

**Date:** November 21, 2026. **Status:** UNAPPROVED quantitative economic model. The user requested an elephant-anchored curve; numerical body mass, daily intake, age-specific survival, solar energy share and acreage are proposals, not canon. Do not replace earlier approved 12-year reproductive/20-year full maturity, 30 seeds, 25% wild/75% captive survival, or 6-year maternal recovery.

## Sources and biological analogues
- Real elephant weight/height growth shows significant expansion through adolescence and after sexual maturity: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4604763/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9341231/ .
- African/Asian calf birth masses often ~90–120kg, dramatically different from Mossback seed proposal: https://ielc.libguides.com/sdzg/factsheets/african_elephant and https://ielc.libguides.com/sdzg/factsheets/asianelephant/reproduction .
- Wild elephant adult dry-matter intake around 1–1.5% of body mass/day, sometimes higher in pregnant/growing specimens: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6361008/ ; https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09712119.2013.875904 .

## Proposed consumption by age (dry-matter energy-equivalent)
- Age 0–5: gross feed need 2.0% of live weight/day; with assumed 50% solar energy credit, purchased/grazed feed equivalent 1.0%/day.
- Age 5–12: gross 1.7%, net 0.85%/day.
- Age 12–20+: gross 1.5%, net 0.75%/day; at 10t mature body 75kg dry-feed equivalent/day, matching prior exploratory adult assumption.
- Feeding rates are NOT validated Mossback measurements; very young milk intake, total lactation requirements, forage protein, feed digestibility, water, exercise and low-light seasonality need separate models. No approved species-wide 50% solar fraction.

## Survival pipeline (unapproved age allocation, satisfying approved overall 75%)
Steady annual births from the illustrative 24-mother, six-cohort nursery: 24 × 30 / (6 years post-Bloom recovery + hypothetical 0.25-year Bloom) = **115.2 seeds/year**. Approved captive survival to age 20 is 75%; propose survival from seed of 90% at age 2, 82% at age 12, and 75% at age 20, interpolated linearly within the 0–2, 2–12 and 12–20 stages for modeling only. Wild survival to age 20 is separately 25%, with no wild age-by-age allocation assumed.

For each year age bin [a,a+1], approximate expected living headcount = annual seed births × modeled survival at a+0.5; mass = linear interpolation of anchor masses at a+0.5; annual dry feed equivalent tonnes = headcount × mass × net feed rate ×365/1000. Round only final figures.

At illustrative usable forage yield **6 tonnes of consumable dry matter per hectare-year**, the whole integrated 0–20-year supply chain needs **~6,222 hectares = 62.2 km² effective productive forage area**, not including paddocks, security perimeters, crop rotation overhead or villages. If the nursery sells all surviving young at age 2, it retains ~219 first two-year juveniles +32 adults and needs ~**1,191t/year** before or **1,430t/year** after 20% allowance; at 6t/ha/y this is ~**238ha** effective forage. Nursery life stages' large feeding demands and all mother-specific lactation/growth demands remain unmodeled in detail.

**IMPORTANT:** This throughput describes a mature steady-state network with a fully populated 20-year inventory, not the first year's adult sales. Eventual annual 20-year survivors = 115.2 × 0.75 = **86.4**, realized only after development lag. At age 12, the proposed model implies 115.2×.82 ≈94.5 surviving animals, potentially reproductively mature but not full-size. They do NOT instantly produce additional offspring unless explicitly entered as additional breeding mothers in a new model, to avoid double-counting.

## Ecological and economic design implications
- The major food bill sits in the 5–20 year grow-out pipeline, not the seed beds.
- Sunrush Grass is **approved as wild prolific bulk forage and an ecological antagonist held in check by Mossback grazing**, but its numerical yield remains unapproved. High-volume Sunrush cannot automatically provide sufficient protein/mineral nutrition; Leshy cultivate supplement crops and manage grazing/land recovery.
- An age-two nursery sale model is one plausible specialized commercial arrangement, not fixed canon. Transfer logistics, consent/coercion and Starspawn memories remain important.
- No currency price, mature 10t norm, market price, feed recipe, sex dimorphism, 50% solar fraction, annual survival-stage breakdown, grow-out labor, milk yield, carcass yield or sanctuary capacity is approved by this draft.
- Strong near-term tests: 25% versus 50% solar energy, heavier adult mass 12t, lower/higher forage usable yield, different juvenile growth timing, and family protective-herd overhead.
