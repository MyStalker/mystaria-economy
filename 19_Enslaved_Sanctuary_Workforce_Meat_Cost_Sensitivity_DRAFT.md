## How much cheaper is *finished meat*?
The full integrated 20-year network has **37,334 t/year mixed-feed provision**, costing ~**1,150,794.11 units/year** with provisional farmgate blend, and yields ~**86.4 age-20 adults/year**, at illustrative **2,800 kg consumed meat per adult** (previous unapproved adult meat yield and 70% Celestial Recall consumption-realization). This full feed bill **already includes nursery feed** of ~44,078.74; avoid adding feed twice.

For minimal **feed + sanctuary nonfeed cost**, annual all-paid reference = 1,150,794.11 + (290,024.61-44,078.74) = **1,396,739.98**, divided by 86.4×2800 = **5.77356 units/kg realized meat**. Moderate captive sanctuary = 1,150,794.11+(177,450.61−44,078.74) = **1,284,165.98**, /241,920 kg = **5.30823 units/kg**. Saving **0.46534 units/kg**, or **8.06% of this *partial*, low-bound meat cost**. Lean ~5.26412 (8.82% reduction); higher-upkeep ~5.35233 (7.30%).

**Critical interpretation:** A ~38.8% sanctuary operating-cost reduction DOES NOT imply ~38.8% cheaper final meat. Most included spending is forage throughout nearly 20 years, and completely unmodeled grow-out ranch wages, pasture/capital costs, transport, butcher margins, animal replacement and retail prices will dilute or modify the true share. A final retail percent CANNOT yet be calculated from sanctuary staffing alone. If every feed and ranch operator is also enslaved, farm costs also change, but must be recomputed by occupation, captive maintenance and specialist productivity; their unchanged prices above do not presume all their workers are paid or freed.

The complete feed bill/86.4 yields 13,319.38 feed-only units per mature animal; the owner's nonfeed nursery burden originally 245,945.87/86.4=2,846.60 units per mature animal versus moderate scenario 133,371.87/86.4=1,543.66. Difference ~1,303 units per mature animal, or ~0.465 per kg. This is not nursery animal transaction price carried wholesale to age 20 and must not be charged twice against integrated annual costs.

## Open issues and realism
- Captive capital purchase/gifting/inheritance, expected lifespan and replacement risk might be large, especially Leshy specialists whose scarcity has already been acknowledged. The moderate 400-unit Leshy annual budget may severely understate this if purchased rather than inherited; require more research.
- 27 ordinary enslaved workers at 700 each could still cost much more/less depending on housing arrangements, age, work schedule, magical sickness and labor skill. If many are part-time, per-worker-year upkeep may double-count resident workers whose main employer is elsewhere; hours per person needed.
- 44 full jobs may already be overstaffed. Neither this comparison nor a low salary solves biological mortality and night shift coverage.
- Sanctuary's 90%-to-age-2 survival assumption remains unapproved. Survival is coupled to skill and welfare; cut costs too deeply and survival may fall, erasing price gains.
- Existing 20% crop markup and breeding markup used on costs, not mathematical profit margins. No final official silver denominations.
- Starspawn unassimilated flesh vanishes on Celestial Recall, so market realized kg needs actual processing/eating within recall timeframe.
