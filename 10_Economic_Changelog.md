# Mystaria Economy: Decision Changelog

## 2026-11-21 — Foundation: Author-described currency, slavery, and non-Hero goals

**Recorded author directions:**
- Three major national currencies plus two minor currencies (Serpentari precious-metal coinage among the minors; second minor undecided).
- Imperium **Scales**: sophisticated paper currency associated with gold-standard banking.
- Canopy Kingdoms: silver primary, gold secondary.
- Scarlands **Vitae**: condensable, industrially useful liquid essence used in Pazara houses and as basis of potion craft. Measured in ounces and colors: **red standard, white middle, pure clear rarest**. **Marks rejected** as a currency name.
- Real Vitae is extracted from living people by alchemists through torturous methods; its supply is affected by extraction and consumption/transformation. Counterfeiters instead make cheaper convincing false Vitae.
- Divine Gems and Glints are exclusive to gods' divine shops, unknown to ordinary non-Heroes without special expertise. Existing Gem tariffs and reward progression preserved.
- Independent non-Hero player progression is an intended experience; an unrelated centaur smith can make QV swords without divine Gems.
- Slavery is exceptionally widespread. Free members of commonly enslaved races are a minority; model free Kharak wages/expenses as a benchmark and enslaved Kharak maintenance/production as a distinct comparison, not a universal wage market.

**Not yet canonized as quantitative rules:** wage schedules, cost-of-living baskets, ounces-to-colors values, Scarlands Vitae extraction yields, silver/gold weights, Scales redemption, Gem-mortal conversion, exact slavery demographics, second minor currency, non-Hero XP/progression formulas.

**Integration:** Cross-reference [mystaria-codex](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex) as mechanical canon and resolve any conflict before claiming new numbers are approved.

## 2026-11-21 — National living standards and Canopy study subjects

**Author-described direction:** Free Imperium residents, even poorer ones, possess near-modern luxuries; Canopy freefolk enjoy idyllic, ordered fantasy prosperity supported by automation and systematic slavery; Scarlands wealth is extremely concentrated and freedom insecure, described as anarchocapitalist in character. First comparative study switches from Kharak to a **middle-class, non-noble free Thornguard** and a **better-off non-noble Thornguard with enslaved Bloomkin**. Wage rates, household size, enslaved-person counts, and precise markets are **OPEN**. See [04 Three National Economies](04_Three_National_Economies.md).

## 2026-11-21 — Thornguard households, Roman analogy and Bloomkin dressmaking

Author sets Canopy Household A as a non-noble, middle-class free Thornguard with single-income family support, purchase of modest home within one year, regular drinks/tournaments, recreational swords and perhaps firearms. Rome-at-its-height is a useful civic/cultural reference; postwar America a purchasing-power analogy. Household B is a somewhat richer Thornguard running a modest dressmaking shop staffed by enslaved Bloomkin, with favorable relative conditions: usually 40-hour workweeks, longer seasonal peaks, sick time, maternity leave, some education, own community, evenings and festivals, casual clothes off duty. Imperium's suggested lower-order enslaved baseline is barracks, assigned uniforms and six-day work, while Scarlands is worse. These are comparative social directions, not guaranteed general laws. Actual numerical budgets, wages, coin conversion, home prices, household size and profitability **OPEN**. Updated `04_Three_National_Economies.md`.

## 2026-11-21 — Canopy town guard and five-Bloomkin dressmaking specifications

**Author-established direction:** Roman-inspired urban rentals and property distribution supersede assuming one-year home purchase universally; postwar US one-income household purchasing power, Roman public amenities, Canopy tournaments and hobbies. Middle-class Thornguard is a noble-house-employed **town guard** with regular hours, five-person household, and his own home life. Prosperous non-noble Thornguard runs a **local dressmaking shop with five enslaved Bloomkin**, local walk-in sales and commissions. Bloomkin more likely **inherited** from aligned noble connections than transferred via conditional loan/patronage; noble houses tend to restrict enslaved-person gifts/trade to allies from professed duty of protection and honor. Canopy race and territory condition legal standing: free Moontouched frequently face noble 'protection' pressure and restrictions in territorial villages, with greater but conditional opportunities in mixed cities. Comparative food affordability: Imperium cheapest aided by Worldspindles, Canopy middle, Scarlands at least double in comparable food cost; dragon preferences affect local taxes. Any precise silver wages, living costs and example budget are **unapproved calibration**, tracked in [05 Canopy Household Budgets — DRAFT](05_Canopy_Household_Budgets_DRAFT.md).

## 2026-11-21 — Author approval: food contingency and managmin nutrition direction

Author approves **10% grocery-cost contingency** and asks that future physical realism studies (spoilage, seasonal availability, farm yields, animal feed, transport, real production costs) be explicitly deferred and recorded. Nutrition in Mystaria is ultimately to be understood through **red, blue, green, white, and black managmins** rather than treating human vitamin/protein/micronutrient requirements as final setting rules. Specific dietary effects or ratios remain **OPEN**. For the unapproved 887.12-unit illustrative annual grocery basket, adding 10% = ~975.83 provisional units, 75.83 above the earlier illustrative 900 budget. No new prices or salaries approved. Updated `05_Canopy_Household_Budgets_DRAFT.md`.

## 2026-11-21 — Author-selected experimental Canopy town demographics and Leshy needs

**Experimental only, not universal demographics:** In the proposed 4,000-person Canopy reference town, **75% (3,000)** are default enslaved residents, principally ~4-foot Moontouched, Tidekin, Bloomkin and Duskpaws; **10% (400) Centaurs** with horse-like food demand without grazing on grass; **10% (400) Thornguard/Thornkin**; **5% (200) photosynthetic Leshy**. Legal status of the separately listed racial categories and 6,000 rural residents remain OPEN. **Approved Leshy ecology:** needs sunlight/light, water and soil; Scarlands alchemists produce artificial daylight for subterranean greenhouses, particularly attractive to sun-averse Vren, without overriding Vren vulnerability.

## 2026-11-21 — Centaur intake raised to horse-scale benchmark for experiment

Author direction raises the reference Centaur diet from provisional 9,000 to approximately **18,000 conventional kcal equivalents/day**, motivated by horse-scale needs, physical work and long working hours. This applies to the **experimental model only**; formal physiology and managmin nutritional mechanics are OPEN. With town composition 3,000 smaller residents, 400 Centaurs, 400 Thornguard and 200 Leshy, intended intake becomes 13.12 million kcal/day and gross annual provisioning including the earlier 30% loss/excess allowance becomes ~6.841 billion kcal equivalent. Centaurs account for ~54.9% of intended intake. Rural population remains unmodeled. Updated `06_Canopy_Settlement_and_Food_Demand_DRAFT.md`.

## 2026-11-21 — Leshy vines and canopy settlements; agricultural workforce experiment

**Author direction incorporated:** Canopy settlements are built in the canopies of enormous trees; Leshy have long reaching body vines for treetop movement and exceptional leverage relative to size. Urban Leshy may maintain living trees, gardens, decorative plantings and household botany. Large enslaved populations grant nobles considerable ability to reassign workforces, though neither frictionless transfers nor universal social/legal status is assumed. The main codex's Leshy reference was updated separately with this anatomy and setting direction, leaving movement and Craft numbers OPEN.

**Unapproved workforce modeling:** For proposed 6,000 rural residents, scenario allocates 3,900 heads across agricultural labor, food processing, forestry, maintenance, transport, domestic care, crafts and services. **1,040 heads** in direct agriculture is a conservative mixed-labor scenario; a highly mechanized/plant-magical comparator has **340 full-year direct farm equivalents**, theoretically releasing 700 other roles while retaining unchanged crop-land target, subject to unknown machinery capability and costs. Countryside 900 Leshy and detailed occupational shares remain entirely hypothetical. See [08 Workforce and Treetop Urbanism — DRAFT](08_Canopy_Workforce_and_Canopy_Urbanism_DRAFT.md).
