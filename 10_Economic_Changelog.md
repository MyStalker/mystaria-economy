# Mystaria Economy: Decision Changelog

## 2026-11-21 — Foundation: Author-described currency, slavery, and non-Hero goals

**Recorded author directions:**
- Three major national currencies plus two minor currencies (Serpentari precious-metal coinage among the minors; second minor undecided).
- Imperium **Scales**: sophisticated paper currency associated with gold-standard banking.
- Canopy Kingdoms: silver primary, gold secondary.
- Scarlands **Vitae**: condensable, industrially useful liquid essence used in Pazara houses and as basis of potion craft. Measured in ounces and colors: **red standard, white middle, pure clear rarest**. **Marks rejected** as a currency name.
- Real Vitae is extracted from living people by alchemists through torturous methods; its supply is affected by extraction and consumption/transformation. Counterfeiters instead make cheaper convincing false Vitae.
- Divine Gems and Glints are exclusive to gods' divine shops, unknown to ordinary non-Heroes without special expertise. Existing Gem tariffs and reward progression preserved.
- Independent non-Hero player progression is an intended experience; an unrelated centaur smith can make QV swords without divine Gems.
- Slavery is exceptionally widespread. Free members of commonly enslaved races are a minority; model free Kharak wages/expenses as a benchmark and enslaved Kharak maintenance/production as a distinct comparison, not a universal wage market.

**Not yet canonized as quantitative rules:** wage schedules, cost-of-living baskets, ounces-to-colors values, Scarlands Vitae extraction yields, silver/gold weights, Scales redemption, Gem-mortal conversion, exact slavery demographics, second minor currency, non-Hero XP/progression formulas.

**Integration:** Cross-reference [mystaria-codex](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex) as mechanical canon and resolve any conflict before claiming new numbers are approved.

## 2026-11-21 — National living standards and Canopy study subjects

**Author-described direction:** Free Imperium residents, even poorer ones, possess near-modern luxuries; Canopy freefolk enjoy idyllic, ordered fantasy prosperity supported by automation and systematic slavery; Scarlands wealth is extremely concentrated and freedom insecure, described as anarchocapitalist in character. First comparative study switches from Kharak to a **middle-class, non-noble free Thornguard** and a **better-off non-noble Thornguard with enslaved Bloomkin**. Wage rates, household size, enslaved-person counts, and precise markets are **OPEN**. See [04 Three National Economies](04_Three_National_Economies.md).

## 2026-11-21 — Thornguard households, Roman analogy and Bloomkin dressmaking

Author sets Canopy Household A as a non-noble, middle-class free Thornguard with single-income family support, purchase of modest home within one year, regular drinks/tournaments, recreational swords and perhaps firearms. Rome-at-its-height is a useful civic/cultural reference; postwar America a purchasing-power analogy. Household B is a somewhat richer Thornguard running a modest dressmaking shop staffed by enslaved Bloomkin, with favorable relative conditions: usually 40-hour workweeks, longer seasonal peaks, sick time, maternity leave, some education, own community, evenings and festivals, casual clothes off duty. Imperium's suggested lower-order enslaved baseline is barracks, assigned uniforms and six-day work, while Scarlands is worse. These are comparative social directions, not guaranteed general laws. Actual numerical budgets, wages, coin conversion, home prices, household size and profitability **OPEN**. Updated `04_Three_National_Economies.md`.

## 2026-11-21 — Canopy town guard and five-Bloomkin dressmaking specifications

**Author-established direction:** Roman-inspired urban rentals and property distribution supersede assuming one-year home purchase universally; postwar US one-income household purchasing power, Roman public amenities, Canopy tournaments and hobbies. ## 2026-11-21 — Canopy becomes equatorial treetop-farming economy; Leshy specialization

**Author's setting decisions:** Canopy Kingdoms lie at the equator, allowing year-round agricultural production. Most farms are in the sunlit upper branches of **enormous trees**, with the dark and dangerous forest floor serving as the source of imported growing soil and other resources. Leshy have woven wheat, vegetables and root crops such as potatoes into living branches. **Provisional Craft targets, not finalized mechanics:** Leshy magic substitutes for fertilizer and poor-soil improvement, allows rare crops on poor substrates (illustrated by a powerful Leshy growing watermelon in desert sand), manages pests and blight, and is at least ~5 ordinary worker-equivalents at planting and plowing **but weak at harvesting**. Specific mana costs, resource conservation and productivity remain OPEN. Author-directed setting updates were also committed to canonical [38 Leshen Foundations](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex/blob/main/38_Leshen_Leshy_Foundations.md), Races, index and main changelog.

**Draft analytical update:** Reinterpret previous 1,529-ha crop area as **effective productive surface**, not automatically 1,529 ha of flat territory. Illustrative 1.0–2.0 ratio of effective sunlit growing area to horizontal canopy projection gives ~15.3–7.6 km² for crop projection, without altering total energy or assuming free light. Under hypothetical 40% planting work of 420 conventional field-worker equivalents and ~5:1 Leshy planting efficiency, ~34 Leshy can cover ~168 planting equivalents, releasing ~134 conventional equivalents; total direct food work ~906 equivalents, not necessarily 340. Both ratios **unapproved**. Livestock pasture assumptions need rework because normal ground grazing may not fit dark dangerous underforest. See [09 Equatorial Treetop Agriculture — DRAFT](09_Equatorial_Treetop_Agriculture_DRAFT.md).

## 2026-11-21 — Canon: Starspawn Recall timeline and fortified livestock economies

**Author approval, operative main-codex owner:** [01 World and Shimmer](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex/blob/main/01_World_and_Shimmer.md) now places ordinary postmortem Starspawn **Recall initiation at approximately 24 hours**, with **~8-hour unrefrigerated fresh-meat spoilage concern**. An exceptionally motivated Starspawn may return to Mystaria after **a season**, while one content in the infinite Celestial Realm may take a year or centuries; reconstituted bodies are depleted of most fat and some muscle, requiring feeding and recuperation. Starspawn experience death and being eaten as horrific, remember suffering, and seek escape from deadly celestial predators. Canopy nonfatal recursive ingredient producers are especially valued; livestock valued for flesh often endure spartan, strictly secured, personhood-neglecting captivity. Giant dangerous stock can breach heavily defended forest-floor ranches, and replacement/capture **Starspawn hunts** are an important cultural practice.

**Important OPEN mechanical/economic issue:** Per existing Astral Heart rules, remaining original body disassembles upon Recall. Which detached, refrigerated, processed, consumed, or partially transformed meat survives after the ~24-hour threshold has **NOT BEEN APPROVED**. Do not use immortal-cow repeated slaughter to explain cheap persistent preserved meat without this ruling. Meat, dairy, egg, fiber costs and ranchland feed demands remain unresolved; previous 30–40 km² total footprint requires revalidation. Economic owning docket updated: [09 Treetop Agriculture Draft](09_Equatorial_Treetop_Agriculture_DRAFT.md). Main codex files updated: `01_World_and_Shimmer.md`, `00_Mystaria_Canon_Index.md`, `10_Canon_Changelog.md`.

## 2026-11-21 — Approved Starspawn meat extinction, retained nourishment, botanical salvage and scarcity target

**Author approval live in [main-codex World and Shimmer](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex/blob/main/01_World_and_Shimmer.md):** All **unassimilated Starspawn meat, including butchered, chilled, cooked or frozen material, vanishes as Recall begins**, approximately 24 hours after an ordinary death. **Previously digested/metabolically assimilated managmins and nourishment stay with the eater.** Refrigeration does not extend Recall; remote communities therefore commonly receive **living Starspawn** for local slaughter, rather than distant frozen shipments. Unrefrigerated spoilage (~8h) separately matters.

**Red-managmin carnivorous plants** can convert some surplus meat into their own retained resources through actual digestion, but with substantial managmin losses and no approved conversion rate or plant lifecycle. Meat remains a culturally/nutritionally essential staple, likely rich in red managmins. User's economic aim is **about 2× as scarce but no more than 4× as expensive relative to an as-yet unspecified historical/reference market baseline**; do not invent exact price inputs or finalize managmin dietary values. Saved full implications and open digestion cutoff in [09 Equatorial Treetop Agriculture Draft](09_Equatorial_Treetop_Agriculture_DRAFT.md). Livestock carrying capacity and cost still need revision.
