# Mystaria: Non-Hero Progression

## Author-established design goal — November 21, 2026
Players should be able to choose non-Hero characters, who may eventually become legends or may never do so. They should have meaningful progression without divinely supplied Hero rewards and guardrails. The experience should feel more immersive and perhaps less tightly balanced.

- A centaur smith may forge a **Quality V sword** while never knowing that divine Gems exist.
- The existing approved skill, crafting, equipment Quality, and character rules in [mystaria-codex](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex) remain authoritative; do not create unsupported replacement advancement formulas.
- Non-Hero progression must not be silently equated to automatic divine Gem rewards.

## Design questions — OPEN
- Which existing attribute, SP, skill and leveling rules apply equally to non-Heroes, and which require explicit adaptation?
- Alternative to patron-qualified XP and divine Gems: ordinary training, profession, practical experience, apprenticeship, commissions or other author-approved mechanisms.
- Equipment accessibility, wealth accumulation, reputation, legal freedom, and racial/institutional restrictions.
- How a non-Hero might transition to Hero status (if at all); avoid assuming this is possible without canon support.

## Proposed analytical separation (not canon mechanics)
1. **Capability progression:** professional proficiency, combat skills, equipment making/use.
2. **Economic progression:** wages, savings, productive tools, ownership, debt and trade networks.
3. **Social progression:** standing, legal status, contacts, privileges, obligations, and freedom.

Do not infer that all races enjoy free access to labor markets: widespread slavery is an essential constraint.
