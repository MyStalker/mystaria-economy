# Noble-Sponsored Meat Day: Canopy Household and Lordly Budgets — DRAFT

**2026-11-21. Canon decision APPROVED, numerical estimates BELOW DRAFT.** User approves widespread Canopy noble-sponsored **Mossback Meat Day** as culturally significant communal institution. Local lords in **most** noble territories pay **about half the price**; for food-insecure families contribute MORE, percentage/access pending future noble territorial finances. Not universal legislation, required attendance, standard weekly schedule or automatically equal distribution across free/enslaved groups. User prioritizes reasonably priced Mossback meals **without disguising the production costs of giant Starspawn**, and notes that economically grounded supporting institutions make extraordinary fantasy ideas viable.

## Institutions and political design
- Customary lord-sponsored **communal public food** aligns with Canopy Oath-of-Protection ideology: ceremonial generosity; patronage, heraldry, feast hall and status; substantial tangible welfare. A lord may pay by cash reimbursement, provide owned herd animals at cost, subsidize market vendors or operate village kitchens, each yields different costs and risks.
- Managed ration tickets or communal tables might help low-income people but raise social, caste, honor and stigma issues; participation by enslaved Bloomkin, Tidekin, Moontouched, other dependent residents is explicitly OPEN and morally consequential. Some nobles might exploit feast access for loyalty or to assert paternalistic authority, while other nobles may be genuinely hospitable; don't assert every noble good or evil.
- Preorders and fixed weekly serving promote high pre-Recall actual assimilation, but digestion time and biological safeguards still bound capacity. Standard weekly frequency and 400g portions remain trial, not established norm.
- Big capital-intensive single-slaughter event needs butcher guilds, safe local refrigerated prep (refrigeration does not delay Recall), several kitchens, rapid delivery, waste-processing, and a sufficiently distributed population. If a town is smaller, multi-hamlet synchronized events may be better, if transit fits Recall window.
- Real-world-style cheese associations and markets can develop through approved persistent living-harvest Starspawn milk but the cheese industry and dairy managmins are next separately owned study, not part of subsidy cost unless priced.
- Culture/name possibilities NOT CANON until user selects: **The Grand Carving**, **The Banner Feast**, **The Lord's Table**, **The Open Board**, **The Common Carving**, **The Feast of the First Cut**, **The Great Portion**, **The Red Table**. Multiple registers: official noble court, plainfolk, festive local nickname. Avoid hard-coding names as established language terms yet.
