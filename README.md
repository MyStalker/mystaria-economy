# Mystaria: The Living Economy

**Status:** Companion economic worldbuilding repository. Author-approved directions are distinguished from economic proposals and unresolved calculations.

This repository develops currencies, markets, labor, industry, trade, household living standards, finance, and non-Hero advancement across Mystaria.

## Authority
- [Main Mystaria Codex](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-codex) is authoritative for approved world lore, rules, equipment Quality, mechanics, divine Gems and Glints, and divine-shop tariffs.
- The author's latest explicit decisions supersede conflicting older discussions.
- This economic repository develops ordinary-world systems without silently altering established codex mechanics.
- Do not assume independent repositories synchronize automatically. On relevant approvals, reconcile any cross-repository references.
- Label hypotheses and numerical examples **PROPOSED** until author approval.

## Scope and design goals
Construct a credible society from production and consumption first, not arbitrary fantasy price lists. Model enslaved and free populations distinctly, with meaningful labor, household costs, property, logistics, monetary scarcity, and regional markets. Make non-Hero play and advancement possible without assuming patron-provided rewards.

**Approval status varies by subject. Consult the current economic index, changelog and owning canon references before applying any wage, price or benchmark. Older draft figures do not supersede later author approvals.**

## Public Living Codex publishing

The [Living Codex](https://resonant-genie-1ee11c.netlify.app/) renders reviewed public sections from this repository. Its [website source and inclusion policy](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-living-codex) remain separate from canon. Approved changes are now collected by the single **Queue reviewed Codex release** workflow in the website repository. It checks hourly, compares generated public output, batches automatic releases at least six hours apart, and defaults to at most 2 production requests per 24 hours and 12 per 30 days. The former per-file build-hook workflow is retired and no longer calls Netlify. Save related approved files together in one atomic commit when possible; do not recreate direct publishing hooks. See [publishing safeguards](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-living-codex/blob/main/docs/PUBLISHING-SAFEGUARDS.md) for dry runs, manual releases and recovery. Private campaign material is excluded, and experiments/proposals retain their approval boundaries. Publishing automation does not approve new canon.
